Gym Timetable

Whether you're stepping into the ring for fitness, youth development, or elite competition training, we have a session for you.

Monday / Wednesday

Junior Boxing Squad

5:00 PM - 6:30 PM

Structured technical boxing development, footwork conditioning, and discipline training for youths.

Tuesday / Thursday

Senior & Competitive Sparring

7:00 PM - 8:30 PM

Advanced technical sparring sessions, stamina conditioning, and competitive bout preparation.

Friday

Open Sparring

6:00 PM - 8:00 PM

Supervised open sparring for registered members and affiliated club visitors.

Saturday

Weekend Warriors

10:00 AM - 11:30 AM

High-intensity pad work, heavy bag drills, and circuit conditioning for all fitness levels.

Note on Subsidies: Session fees are heavily subsidized for local youth. First-time visitors must complete a registration form and parental waiver (if under 18) before participating.
