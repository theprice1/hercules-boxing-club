Welfare & Protection

Safeguarding Policy

Creating a secure, respectful, and inclusive environment for all junior and senior members.

Our Commitment

Hercules Boxing Club CIC acknowledges its duty of care to safeguard and promote the welfare of children, young people, and vulnerable adults. As we prepare for our official England Boxing affiliation, we are fully committed to ensuring that every participant takes part in boxing in a safe, positive, and harassment-free environment.

Core Principles

  • The welfare of children and young people is paramount.
  • All participants, regardless of age, gender, background, or ability, have a right to equal protection from abuse.
  • A zero-tolerance approach toward bullying, discrimination, or improper conduct of any kind.
  • Mandatory background checks (DBS) for all coaches, trainers, and regular volunteers.

Club Welfare Officer

Every affiliated club requires a designated point of contact for welfare concerns. Our Club Welfare Officer oversees all reporting procedures, ensures compliance with national governing body guidelines, and provides a confidential channel for members or parents to raise issues.
