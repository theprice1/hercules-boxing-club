More Than Just a Gym

As a registered Community Interest Company (CIC), Hercules Boxing Club exists to serve the Islington community. We are actively working towards our official England Boxing affiliation to elevate our athletes to the national stage.

Youth Subsidies

We ensure that cost is never a barrier. Our session fees are heavily subsidized to protect and empower disadvantaged local youth.

Competitive Pathway

Preparing to step onto the national stage. Our upcoming affiliation allows our boxers to officially compete in tournaments.

Safe Environment

Committed to excellence in welfare. We adhere strictly to national safeguarding policies to protect all our members.
