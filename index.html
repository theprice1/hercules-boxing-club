Our Community Mission

Hercules Boxing Club operates as a not-for-profit Community Interest Company, making elite-level coaching accessible to everyone in Islington.

Competitive Pathway

Preparing to step onto the national stage with structured amateur bouts and elite sparring camps.

Safe Environment

Committed to excellence in welfare. We adhere strictly to national safeguarding policies for youth and vulnerable adults.

Youth Subsidies

We ensure that cost is never a barrier. Our session fees are heavily subsidized to support local Islington families.
