# Hercules Boxing Club CIC: Digital Handover & Operations Guide

Welcome to the official technical handover guide for the Hercules Boxing Club website repository. This document outlines how the site is structured, how to make updates without a technical background, and how your digital ecosystem is set up to support your transition to an **England Boxing Affiliated Club**.

---

## 📂 1. Repository & Project Structure

The website has been built as a lightweight, lightning-fast static site. This ensures **zero ongoing hosting costs** and maximum security.

```text
hercules-boxing-club/
├── index.html           # Homepage, Hero, & Core CIC Mission
├── about.html           # Detailed CIC background for grant bodies
├── timetable.html       # Responsive squad training schedule
├── safeguarding.html   # England Boxing welfare compliance
├── contact.html         # Gym location, address, and enquiry form
├── css/                 # Custom styling overrides
├── js/                  # Mobile navigation drawer interaction
└── assets/              # Logos, images, and brand

🛠️ 2. How to Update Content (No Coding Required)
You do not need to install complex software to update text on the website. Because the repository lives on GitHub, your team can make minor text changes directly through your web browser:

Log into your club’s GitHub account and open the hercules-boxing-club repository.

Click on the file you want to update (e.g., timetable.html or contact.html).

Click the pencil icon (Edit this file) in the top right of the file view.

Make your text adjustments (like changing a class time or email address).

Scroll to the bottom, click Commit changes, and the live site will automatically update within minutes!

📱 3. Connecting Your Social Media Channels
The website footer currently features placeholder badges for Instagram (IG), TikTok, and Facebook. Once your official social handles are active, you can link them up:

Open the HTML files (or use the GitHub web editor).

Locate the <footer> section at the bottom of the code.

Replace the placeholder <span> tags with standard anchor links (<a href="YOUR_URL" target="_blank">...</a>).

🛡️ 4. Grant & Governance Readiness
To assist with your upcoming grant applications and England Boxing Affiliation, the site has been specifically pre-configured with:

CIC Status Transparency: Highlighting your community interest mission on the homepage and About page.

Youth Subsidies Messaging: Explicitly stating your commitment to keeping fees affordable for disadvantaged local youth.

Safeguarding Compliance: A dedicated welfare page outlining your duty of care and compliance with national governing body standards.

🤝 5. Support & Maintenance
This repository is completely open, transparent, and yours to own. If you ever need assistance scaling the site up, adding a member portal, or expanding your digital footprint, feel free to reach out to your digital volunteer team!
```
