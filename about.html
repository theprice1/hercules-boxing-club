Our Mission & Impact

Rooted in Islington. Changing lives through discipline, fitness, and structured sport.

Who We Are

Hercules Boxing Club CIC is more than just a gym—it is a community anchor in the heart of Islington. Operating as a registered Community Interest Company, our core objective is social impact. We use the sweet science of boxing to instill discipline, mental resilience, and physical health into local residents, with a heavy emphasis on shielding disadvantaged youth from antisocial behavior.

Right now, we are operating as a local hidden gem, but our sights are firmly set on national expansion. By upgrading our institutional infrastructure and securing official recognition, we are building a sustainable pathway from our gym floor to official championship rings.

01

England Boxing Affiliation

We are actively completing our formal transition to become an England Boxing affiliated club. This critical milestone will grant our boxers full clearance to enter official regional and national tournaments, wear official competition cards, and represent Islington proudly on the national stage.

02

Funding & Youth Subsidies

As a CIC, every penny we secure goes right back into the gym. Grants and external funding bodies allow us to keep our session fees heavily subsidized. This ensures that financial barriers never prevent a dedicated young person from stepping through our doors.

Partnering With Grant Bodies & Authorities

We welcome inquiries from local authorities, sports councils, and corporate grant providers. Our digital infrastructure, governance frameworks, and safeguarding policies are fully transparent to support funding applications for kits, travel expenses, and facility upgrades.
