Our Mission & Impact

Rooted in Islington. Changing lives through discipline, fitness, and structured sport.

Who We Are

Hercules Boxing Club operates as a registered Community Interest Company (CIC) dedicated to serving the Islington community through accessible, high-standard amateur boxing and fitness coaching.

Our vision is to bridge social gaps, foster community cohesion, and empower youth and adults alike through structured athletic development.

01

Community First

Ensuring that cost is never a barrier to participation with heavily subsidized youth sessions and open community access.

02

Elite Pathways

Providing top-tier technical coaching and structured competition pathways for athletes aiming to step onto the regional and national stage.

Partnering With Grant Bodies & Authorities

We actively collaborate with local authorities, community trusts, and funding partners to expand our reach, improve facility infrastructure, and deliver impactful social outcomes across Islington.
